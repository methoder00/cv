01 / ROBOT LEARNING · 2026

DPP

Dynamic Manipulation with World-Action Models via Counterfactual Planning

Sunwoo Park*, Wonbin Lee*, Seonghyun Jin*, Youngmin Kim*, Jangho Park, Jong Chul Ye

arXiv preprint · Co-first authorPaper ↗Project & demos ↗
View figure ↗

Dynamic Predictive Planning reuses manipulation skills learned from static demonstrations to interact with moving targets, without additional training on dynamic data.

  • Problem: World-action models can lose responsiveness to target relocation as execution advances, even when the required manipulation skill is already learned.
  • Approach: Predict interaction timing and target motion, synthesize a counterfactual RGB-D observation in a familiar robot context, then connect the canonical plan to the actual robot state and refine the remaining actions.
  • Evaluation: Simulation in RoboTwin 2.0 covers bimanual grasping, pick-and-place, and extended multi-stage tasks. Separate real-robot experiments evaluate moving-object pickup and ball interception.

02 / VIDEO GENERATION · 2026

CRePE

Curved Ray Expectation Positional Encoding for Unified-Camera-Controlled Video Generation

Seonghyun Jin*, Youngmin Kim*, Sunwoo Park*, Jong Chul Ye

NeurIPS 2026 · Co-first authorPaper ↗
View figure ↗

A geometric interface for video world models that unifies camera motion, lens geometry, and scene structure beyond conventional pinhole cameras.

  • Problem: Camera encodings based only on ray direction do not locate scene content along a ray, and pinhole assumptions limit control under wide-angle and fisheye lenses.
  • Approach: Represent each image token as a depth-aware distribution along its Unified Camera Model ray and integrate rotary positional information along the curved path in a query view.
  • Model: A lightweight Geometric Attention Adapter on a frozen video diffusion transformer, with geometry supervision during training.
  • Outcome: Improved camera and lens fidelity across pinhole, wide-angle, and fisheye settings, with zero-shot transfer to unseen real videos and geometry-conditioned generation through Radial MixForcing.

03 / 4D GENERATION · 2026

G4S

Geometric 4D Stitching for Grounded 4D Generation

Sunwoo Park, Taesung Kwon, Jong Chul Ye

arXiv preprint · First authorPaper ↗
View figure ↗

An explicit, geometry-aware framework for completing missing regions of dynamic scenes and building expandable 4D representations.

  • Problem: Generative scene completion can introduce inconsistent geometry, while radiance-based reconstruction requires expensive optimization.
  • Approach: Identify missing geometric regions, refine generated target-view geometry using source-view anchors, and stitch reliable newly revealed regions into an explicit 4D mesh.
  • Outcome: Improved geometric consistency, iterative scene expansion, novel-view rendering, and 4D scene editing.
  • Reported runtime: Under 10 minutes on one NVIDIA RTX 5090 for one-step scene expansion, under the paper's experimental setup.
