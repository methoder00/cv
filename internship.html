01 / ROBOT LEARNING · 2026

DPP

Dynamic Manipulation with World-Action Models via Counterfactual Planning

Sunwoo Park*, Wonbin Lee*, Seonghyun Jin*, Youngmin Kim*, Jangho Park, Jong Chul Ye

arXiv preprint · Co-first authorPaper ↗Project & demos ↗
View figure ↗

DPP enables dynamic manipulation with world-action models trained on static demonstrations. It estimates when an interaction will occur and where a moving target will be, then constructs a counterfactual observation that places the predicted target in a familiar robot context. The generated plan is connected to the robot's actual state and refined during execution, improving performance in simulation and real-robot experiments without additional training on dynamic data.

02 / VIDEO GENERATION · 2026

CRePE

Curved Ray Expectation Positional Encoding for Unified-Camera-Controlled Video Generation

Seonghyun Jin*, Youngmin Kim*, Sunwoo Park*, Jong Chul Ye

NeurIPS 2026 · Co-first authorPaper ↗
View figure ↗

CRePE introduces a depth-aware positional encoding for camera-controlled video generation across pinhole, wide-angle, and fisheye lenses. It models each image token as a distribution along its camera ray and integrates positional information along the curved path induced in another view. A lightweight adapter applies this encoding to a frozen video diffusion transformer, improving camera and lens fidelity while supporting zero-shot transfer and externally supplied geometry for scene-conditioned generation.

03 / 4D GENERATION · 2026

G4S

Geometric 4D Stitching for Grounded 4D Generation

Sunwoo Park, Taesung Kwon, Jong Chul Ye

arXiv preprint · First authorPaper ↗
View figure ↗

G4S builds geometrically grounded 4D scenes by completing only missing geometric regions. It refines geometry inferred from generated target views using source-view geometry as an anchor, then stitches reliable newly revealed regions into an explicit dynamic mesh. This region-based process improves geometric consistency and reduces reconstruction cost, enabling iterative scene expansion, novel-view rendering, and 4D editing.

04

Research Interests / Work in Progress

AutoBot Ongoing research · Planned submission to CVPR 2027

AutoBot investigates a harness connecting vision-language models for embodied reasoning with vision-language-action models and world-action models. The goal is to combine long-horizon planning and 3D spatial understanding with precise, contact-rich robot skills, addressing the impact of embodied reasoning model inference latency on execution and its limitations in demanding motor tasks. This ongoing work explores coordination between high-level reasoning and learned action generation, with simulation-assisted evaluation guiding adaptation to unfamiliar tasks.
